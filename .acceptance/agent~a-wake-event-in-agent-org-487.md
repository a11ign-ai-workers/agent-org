A wake event in the trace store says what the wake did, `wrote`, `no-op` or `escalated` (or `null` when the store cannot tell), and which run did it (agent-org#487, Move 0 of a11ign/a11ign#4505).

**Measure first, as the row asked, and what the measurement changed.** The row's first candidate source is the `gh` ledger. Over the last 7 days (the store at 2026-10-10 23:2x UTC, 5,566 wake events), a derivation from the ledger alone (write verb in the session's window, else no-op) settled 73% of a seeded draw of 200 and called 10 of them `no-op`. Checked against the transcripts' own command text, the ledger alone is not enough, three ways, each measured and each now in the code: (1) 52 of the 321 wakes it called `no-op` had committed, pushed or edited a file (46 of 173 worker wakes), which is in no `gh` ledger, so a turn now carries `repoWrite`; (2) the ledger has holes that read as a quiet session (2026-10-09T19 to 10-10T06 holds 0 to 22% of the calls the rollup counted, by hour; inside 10-09T04 one ten-minute bucket holds 119 calls against 780 to 1,110 in the others, behind an hour whose count is met), so an hour count is a screen and not a proof: a turn now carries `ghCommands`, and a window whose text issued `gh` and whose ledger holds none of the session's calls is `null`; with the hour count alone 4 of 130 told `no-ops` were writes the transcript shows, with both 0 of 123; (3) `prompt:session` is no `gh` call, so the sender is read from the two send logs, of which about a fifth carry no sender, and a send of an unnamed sender in a window makes it `null`.

**What changes.**
- `store.ts`: `outcome` and `runId` on a wake (`wakeOutcomes`, pure), `repoWrite` and `ghCommands` on a turn (`repoWriteOf`, `ghCommandsOf`), `sendsOfLogs`, `countedHours`, `outcomeSplit`, and the DEFINITIONS entry.
- `trace.ts`: `settleWakeOutcomes` (the ingest step: reads the ledgers and their `.hourly` rollups and the two send logs, writes one batch once the oldest settle-able wake is 3 hours old, writes nothing on a host with no ledger, and leaves every wake as it was when a source cannot be read) and `trace -- --wake-outcomes [--since] [--until] [--store] [--json 1]`, which prints the split per cause with `null` on its own line.
- `ingest-state.ts`: `STATE_VERSION` 6, so stored turns get the two new fields (see Outside-Region).
- `wake.ts` is not touched: nothing in it needed to change.

**Measured, and how.** On a COPY of the live store (`cp` to the scratchpad, `--store <copy>`, never the shared file), after a cold re-read of 2.6 GB of transcripts (48, 54 and 105 s on three runs): of 5,566 wakes of the last 7 days `wrote` 4,447 (79.9%), `no-op` 123 (2.2%), `escalated` 2 (0.0%), `null` 994 (17.9%), 6 of those not yet settled. Managers (ceo, product-manager, orchestrator, 2,975 wakes): `wrote` 81.5%, `no-op` 1.2%, `null` 17.2%. A seeded draw of 200 wakes (seed 487, 3 hours or older, 5,447 in the pool): 165 told (157 `wrote`, 8 `no-op`), 35 `null`; every told wake checked against the transcript text of its own window, 0 contradicted. All 123 `no-op` and both `escalated` of the window checked the same way, 0 contradicted. 456 wakes ran a `prompt:session` in their window; 370 of them also wrote and are `wrote`, 84 are `null`, 2 are `escalated`.

**The 39% does not reproduce under this definition.** The epic's "39% of manager wakes write nothing" is, here, 1.2% `no-op` (1.5% of the told ones), and edit-only wakes (`issue edit`, `pr edit`, `pr ready` and nothing else) are 5% of the manager `wrote`s, so label edits do not explain the gap. `wrote` here is any write verb in the window, and `issue comment` is in 1,784 of the manager windows. Whatever the 39% counted, it was a narrower write than this; the field is the reading, and what `wrote` should mean is `ceo`'s.
